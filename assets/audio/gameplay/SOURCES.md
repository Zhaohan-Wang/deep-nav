# Gameplay audio sources

The committed OGG files are trimmed, level-matched runtime assets rather than the
original downloads.

- `rocket_explosion.ogg` — user-provided `i-need-a-really-short-fhfxulua.wav`
- `rocket_engine_loop.ogg` — user-provided `strong-rocket-engine-soun-xr7fyuz5.wav`
- `rocket_thrust_transition.ogg` — user-provided
  `freesound_community-woosh-104586.mp3`; its initial 0.48 seconds of silence
  were removed
- `rocket_turn_burst.ogg` — user-provided
  `freesound_community-rocket-landing-38715.mp3`; trimmed to its active
  3.60-second section
- `ship_impact.ogg`, `mission_complete.ogg`, `ui_click.ogg`,
  `relay_reached.ogg`, and `system_alert.ogg` — reused from the local
  `dyadic-force` project's selected SFX set

The 3:21 `Rocket Science - Cinematic Hybrid Orchestral Instrumental Beat Strings
Trailer (Preview).mp3` is intentionally not used.
